1. `--budget $50 --3d --feedback The game is Central Park Pigeon. The idea is that you're a piegon flying around Central Park, exploring, eating food, etc. You decide it's time to take over the park, the city, and maybe the world. The goal of the game is to befriend other animals in the park -- other birds, racoons, squirrels, whatever -- and get them to help you. They each help in different creative ways -- crows can help you trade items with humans to get gear you can't normally get, racoons can rig human vehicles so you can drive them, squirrels and find things underground, ducks can get you things from under the water, etc. You can recruit pigeions and have them flock with you. You can walk around and fly around. Ultimately the game is a big puzzle, though you may fight owls, hawks, angry humans, dogs, etc., at times. Mostly puzzly.
