# Fix run: #8, #16, #19, #23 (2026-11-22)

The person's own issues, from playing the published game. Their words are in design/STATUS.md (requests 22–23) and on GitHub. Planned changes below; the design review appends its findings at the end.

## #8: Report a bug with a controller (bug)

Report: with a controller, nothing closes the report form (a softlock until reload), and pressing ✕ in the title field brings up no keyboard, so nothing can be typed.

- Back (B / ○), and Esc, always close the form, from any field. Focus can always reach Cancel.
- The D-pad and stick move between the form's parts. ✕ / A presses the focused button.
- A controller can't type in a browser, so the form gets one-press choices ("I'm stuck", "Something looks wrong", "Confusing", "Other"). Picking one fills the title. Sending works with no typing; the screenshot and game details go with it. A keyboard player can still type.
- Leaving the form puts the game back where it was (paused), with input working.

## #16: Peas give no feedback (bug)

Report: they flew to the table with the peas, pressed □ (Peck) next to it and heard a sound, but nothing showed whether they'd picked up peas. Back at the ducks, nothing happened.

- First, reproduce it and find out whether the pickup actually fails (wrong spot, wrong button, a step order).
- Then: a clear pickup (a "Got peas" moment and a carried-peas icon), ducks that react when you arrive carrying peas, and a hint if the press does nothing (say why).
- Keep the puzzle's twist: the fun pass made the swarm fail out loud and a FEED sign lure the picnic off its tub.

## #19: The Hot Anchovy slice (changes play)

Report and comments:
- The slice you pick up is clearly pepperoni, but Queenie asks for anchovy.
- After failing, the slice is stuck on your face with no obvious way to drop it.
- The top-left label says "hot" when the slice is clearly cold.
- The cart man stands in the middle of the street; the carts are hard to land on, and it's unclear how to rewarm a slice ("it should just work on any food cart").
- Ideas offered: give the slice to a person or animal who'd happily eat it, or a Drop button while carrying. Dropping should reset the puzzle so you can steal it again and try the grates.

Planned:
- **Anchovy look:** the den-cats slice gets anchovies. Edit the slice model's own texture with nanogen, keep its UV layout, about $0.20, and use it only for the anchovy slice. Other slices stay pepperoni.
- **Label:** the carried label says Hot, Warm or Cold from the slice's age, the same rule Queenie uses.
- **Getting rid of it:** while carrying anything, a Drop prompt shows. Near someone who'd eat it (a person on a bench, a rat, a raccoon, a dog), the prompt is Give: they take it with a line, and it's gone. Dropping or giving away the anchovy slice resets the puzzle: a fresh slice is put out on the sill (the existing 60 s refill, sooner after a drop), with a one-line prompt.
- **Rewarming:** any food cart warms a slice when Gus is within about 3 m of the cart, perched or hovering, with no landing needed. A short sizzle and a "Warm again!" toast. When the slice goes cold while carried, the goal card adds "Cold. Any food cart will warm it." Move the halal cart man (and his cart, if needed) onto the pavement.
- Keep: the grate relay (still the clever route), Pickles waking up and pushing the slice off, and the anchovy fan red herring.

## #23: Puzzles must stay solvable (changes play)

Report: "You shouldn't be able to fail a mission in a way that makes it so you can never do it. Ideally the mission would just prompt and set up again."

- Audit all 31 schemes plus the finale for failure states that leave a step impossible. For example: an item lost, spent or destroyed with no respawn; an NPC moved or gone for good; a one-time trigger used up; a timer that can run out for good; a vehicle wrecked or stranded; a phase-locked window that never comes back; leaving mid-attempt.
- Every failure resets the puzzle's own setup (the item respawns, NPCs go back to their places, the trigger re-arms) with a short line, so the player can try again. Never a dead end.
- Also check that old saves already in a dead end are repaired on Continue: extend G.schemes.repairSpent.
- design/reviews/fixes-3-midscene-sweep.md already covered quitting mid-scene; this is about failing in play.
- The deliverable includes a table: scheme, how it can fail, what resets it, and how it was proven.

## Design review (round 1)

(appended below)

Two reviewers (Player, Builder) read the plan against the code. Decisions (these override the plan above where they differ):

**#8**
- The softlock is input.js:162: while the form is open, the game throws away ALL controller input, B included. Fix: keep tracking button states but skip game actions. B (button 1) calls the form's close() (feedback.md:117-120). Consume that press so it doesn't also close Pause on the next frame.
- vendor/gh-feedback.js is out of date with the gamegen template (feedback.md says copy it unchanged). Re-copy it, then re-check what report.js patches (the `heading` attribute may replace the rename hack).
- D-pad focus and one-press choices are done from the game side (the form's open shadow root, or a game-drawn quick menu). Choices: I'm stuck · A button doesn't work · Something looks wrong · Confusing · I lost progress · Other. Focus starts on the first choice.
- Be honest about sending: the form runs in link mode (opens GitHub). A controller press isn't a browser "user gesture", so window.open and the clipboard can be blocked. Test it. If it's blocked, the controller path says plainly "Opens GitHub to finish: use a mouse, touch or keyboard for this last step", and the report waits for that click. Never promise a screenshot or a send it can't make.

**#16**
- Not a broken pickup. The picnickers were still seated, so the guard refused (den-ducks.js:56-62). The refusal is a small bubble above a picnicker plus a blip, and later presses give only the blip.
- Fix: guards return a reason, and pickUp shows it at Gus as a toast every time, hinting without giving the answer ("They won't leave it while they're sat there. Something has to get them up."). The prompt reads "Guarded: …".
- Outside the Afternoon, Peck at the empty picnic spot says "The picnic's here in the afternoons".
- Ducks react (a bubble or chirp, non-blocking) when Gus arrives carrying peas. A clear "Got peas!" toast on pickup.

**#19**
- **Give is for slices only,** on Peck near an eater (a bench person, a rat, a raccoon, a dog). Drop (Q / L1 / long-press) stays a plain drop. Existing takers and scheme hand-ins always win (takers.js:70-80: Queenie, Sue, the stage guard, Walter, Vinnie, Big Sal, Mama Mask). No Give within about 8 m of Queenie while carrying the anchovy slice.
- **Drop does not delete the slice.** The griddle route drops it on purpose. Losing or getting rid of the anchovy slice (dropping it out of reach, Give, a nap, Ruffled) sets the sill refill timer early. The prompt names the place and marks it. The sill only refills Morning to Dusk, so at Night say "Out again in the morning". After the first push, Pickles gives way quickly ("*sigh* … fine.").
- **Rewarming: not at every food cart.** Any cart within 3 m of Queenie would remove the puzzle; the 820 m problem is the clever part. The halal griddles warm the slice when Gus hovers or perches within about 3 m, with no landing needed. Use the same 3 s rule and emit `sliceWarmed`. Keep warming by dropping the slice on the griddle (den-cats.alt depends on it). Exclude Vinnie's cart (part-body.alt) and the ice-cream truck. A cold carried slice adds to the goal card: "Cold. The halal cart's griddle will warm it" (with a compass mark). The report must say this deliberately narrows the owner's "any food cart" idea, and why.
- **The cart man:** move the man onto the pavement. Move the cart (SLICE.griddle) only if it's really in the road. It's read by items.js, street.js, map.js, goals-ssf.js and people.js, and den-cats.alt would need re-recording.
- **Label:** Hot / "Cooling, still counts" / Cold, where Cold is exactly !isHot. Also fix hud.js:104-116: the suffix is only written when the ring value changes.
- **Anchovy look:** a second texture (pizza-slice-anchovy), swapped into mat.map for items with id slice-anchovy, and for the anchovy fan's prop. Pull the base texture out of the GLB, edit it with nanogen keeping the UV islands, and check it on the model. Fallback if that fails: make the code anchovies (items.js:87) about 4× bigger.
- All new cues are toasts or bubbles, never G.say or a cutscene, so replays don't lose Peck presses to dialogue.

**#23**
- Audit every scheme plus the cross-scheme rules that lose items: naps (items.js:300), Ruffled (:305-316), drops over water (prove they can be picked up again), and the new Give.
- Rule: a quest item dropped out of reach, or left more than about 90 s away from where it's needed, floats back to its source (the same idea as Ruffled).
- Add a check-schemes rule: every needed item has a source that comes back (a per-phase clue, a spawner, or a respawn when lost).
- Extend repairSpent: den-ducks step 3 with no peas anywhere → respawn the tub (and the same for any item found in the audit).
- Deliverable: the table (scheme, how it can fail, what resets it, proof).
