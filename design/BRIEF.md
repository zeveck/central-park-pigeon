# Central Park Pigeon: Brief

**In one sentence:** you're a scrappy Central Park pigeon who flies, waddles and schemes across a big, recognisable 3D Central Park (and the city block around it), befriending its animals, winning over its pigeon gangs and gathering parts for a giant Mecha-Pigeon that ends up on top of the Empire State Building yelling "FEED THE BIRDS".

**The feeling:** cheeky, sunny, free. The joy of flight over a place you know, plus the "aha!" of a clever plan coming together. It's a comedy heist with feathers. Fights happen, but they're short, silly scrapes, not the point.

## What they asked for

- [ ] You're a pigeon, **flying around Central Park**, exploring, **eating food**.
- [ ] You **decide to take over the park, the city, and *maybe* the world** (the escalating ambition is the story's spine; "maybe the world" is loose).
- [ ] **Befriend other animals**: other birds, raccoons, squirrels, "whatever" (open list, loose), and they **help you in different creative ways**:
  - [ ] **Crows** help you **trade items with humans** to get gear you can't normally get.
  - [ ] **Raccoons** **rig human vehicles so you can drive them**.
  - [ ] **Squirrels** **find things underground**.
  - [ ] **Ducks** **get things from under the water**.
  - [ ] "etc.": more allies welcome (loose).
- [ ] **Recruit pigeons and have them flock with you.**
- [ ] **Walk around and fly around.**
- [ ] **Ultimately a big puzzle.** *Mostly puzzly.*
- [ ] You **may fight owls, hawks, angry humans, dogs, etc.**, "at times" (fights are occasional).
- [ ] Change **"Don't Feed the Birds" signs to "Feed the Birds"** → nearby humans feed you.
- [ ] **Park personnel eventually change the signs back, UNLESS raccoons sabotage their cars**, etc.
- [ ] The park has **multiple "gangs" of pigeons**; win them **all** to your side.
- [ ] **Recruit all the denizens of the park** to your cause.
- [ ] **Gather parts to build the Mecha-Pigeon**, a giant pigeon that can rule over the park.
- [ ] **Ending:** the Pigeon **climbs to the top of the Empire State Building** and **shouts "FEED THE BIRDS"**.
- [ ] **Mostly a fun exploring-the-park game.**
- [ ] A **large and fairly accurate Central Park map, with iconic locations**.
- [ ] **"Maybe" a block of NYC around it** in every direction (loose), so you can **fly up a building and perch**, with **"maybe" items and friends** out there too (loose).
- [ ] The name: **Central Park Pigeon**.

## Feedback on the shared design (requests #2–#6)

- [ ] **#2 A sweeping feel, not a handful of characters:** "a bunch of crows/squirrels/ducks/etc., but you only interact with a subset directly"; "just a few named individuals per species".
- [ ] **#3 More species:** "Rats and turtles are great!" (keep them); "also include **cats**, **horses**, and **other species of birds**."
- [ ] **#4 "Think about Central Park and NYC."** (real park and city life, places and culture; original characters, no brands or real people)
- [ ] **#5 Pizza:** "nab and **carry slices of pizza** from place to place", "to **bribe raccoons or even people** to get them to **open a gate** or something."
- [ ] **#6 Parallel puzzles:** the sign war "was meant to be an example"; "a few different **overlapping puzzles** that you're kinda **solving in parallel** as you explore and befriend."
- [ ] **Later notes:** don't explode the size ("a bit more oomph", "a bit bigger" is fine); keep it fun and not too complicated: a casual player should enjoy exploring, making friends and solving things, and be able to beat it ("not hard like Myst"), but with clever bits, not just obvious fetch quests ("The FUN is what matters").
- [ ] **Platform:** desktop-first (keyboard and mouse, plus controller); phones best-effort.

## Who plays, where, how long

- Players who like cosy exploration games with light puzzles and slapstick; all ages (cartoon peril, no gore).
- **Desktop first** (keyboard and mouse), plus **a PS5 or other controller** (`--gamepad`); phones best-effort. Web, in the browser, installable.
- A full playthrough of a **large** game: about 1.5 to 2 hours. Sessions of 10 to 30 minutes, saving often, so you can stop anywhere.

## Style references

None were given. The look gamegen chose: **a sunny, chunky, toy-like 3D park**. Rounded low-poly shapes, soft painted textures, warm late-afternoon light, big readable silhouettes. Characters are expressive and a little cartoonish, like vinyl toys or a stop-motion film. Borrow the *mood* of cosy open-world animal games (a small creature in a big familiar place, gentle mischief, a checklist of friends to make). Copy nothing: no characters, names, art or music from any game or film. The finale nods to the classic "giant creature on the Empire State Building" movie image, but the scene, characters and staging are our own.

## Settings and decisions made on the person's behalf

- 3D web game in three.js; desktop + phones; controller support; in-game "Report a bug" button (on).
- Budget $50 for all models, pictures, music and sound, with $5 of mock pictures for the shared design now. Plan only: nothing else is spent until `--continue`.
- **Large scope**, judged from the request.
- The map is **"fairly accurate," not 1:1**. Central Park is 4 km long; flying that at bird speed would be a long, empty commute. Landmarks keep their real order and relative places, south to north and east to west, with the distances between them squeezed so the whole park flies end to end in about a minute and a half.
- **Fights are short and dodge-and-outwit**, never health-bar slogs, so the game stays "mostly puzzly."
- The finale's climber is **the Mecha-Pigeon, piloted by you**. The request says "the Pigion"; the giant robot pigeon is the payoff of the whole parts quest, and it's the more iconic image.
